**PropTrack keeps a property agent's clients and viewings in one place.** It is a desktop app for agents who
prefer typing to clicking: every action is a short command, and the window shows your clients, the selected
client's details and your schedule side by side.

With PropTrack you can:

* **Keep a client directory.** Record each buyer, seller, landlord or tenant with their phone, address,
  optional email and tags, and find them again by name, phone number or client type.
* **Plan your appointments.** Book viewings, signings and handovers against a client. PropTrack rejects
  double bookings, and you can list your schedule by client or by day.
* **Recover from mistakes.** Undo and redo recent changes, and back up or restore your whole client list
  and schedule from a file.

PropTrack runs on Windows, macOS and Linux with Java 25, and keeps your data in a local file on your
computer.

* To start using PropTrack, head over to the [_Quick start_ section of the **User Guide**](UserGuide.html#quick-start).
